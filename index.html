🏛️ Topic 4: Early Hong Kong Region / 早期香港地區的歷史、文化與傳承

Part 1: The Four Settlers / 四大民系
Most of the early settlers in Hong Kong belonged to the four major groups: the Cantonese, the Hakkas, the Fuklos and the boat people. Some of them could possibly have settled along the coast in Hong Kong since the early days, while some of them came from the mainland.
今日香港大部分居民的來源，可以分為四大系統，即廣府人、客家人、福佬人和水上人。這四大民系，有的可能早就生活在香港沿海地區，有的則是從內地遷徙而來。

⭐ NT Five Great Clans / 新界五大姓族

Origins: The ancestors of the Cantonese came from central and northern China. They first moved into Guangdong during the Han dynasty. Later, they started coming to Hong Kong from the Song dynasty.
Identity: Since they settled in Hong Kong a long time ago, they were also called the Puntis, which means 'local people'. They mainly spoke Cantonese, and most of them were farmers.
Five Clans: Tang, Man, Liu, Hau and Pang. All of them are Cantonese, speak Cantonese, and settled in HK since the Song dynasty.
起源與背景： 廣府人的祖先原來是中原人士，漢朝以後陸續南遷至廣州府各地，宋朝以後又逐批移民到香港地區。
稱呼與工作： 由於他們早就遷居於香港，所以習慣上又稱為「本地人」（Puntis）。他們操粵語（廣東話），在古代以耕種為生。
新界五大姓族： 包括鄧、文、廖、侯、彭五姓族，他們均屬於廣府人，操粵語，而且都是宋朝以來先後從中國內地遷居於香港的。
Part 2: What is Intangible Cultural Heritage? / 甚麼是非物質文化遺產？
Cultural heritage refers to traditions kept by a local community. Intangible cultural heritage (ICH) includes traditional customs, festivals, songs, techniques, etc.
文化遺產，又稱為文化承傳，是指一個本地社群保存下來的傳統。無形的文化遺產稱為「非物質文化遺產」，包括傳統習俗和節日、歌曲、工藝技術等。

Part 3: Cheung Chau Jiao (Bun) Festival / 長洲太平清醮

📅 Specific Date Milestone / 舉辦日期之重要考點

Since 2001, the day of the Cheung Chau Jiao Festival has been fixed on the 8th day of the fourth lunar month, which is also the Birthday of the Buddha holiday.

由2001年起，長洲太平清醮的正日被定為農曆四月初八（即佛誕公眾假期）。

🌿 Jiao Festival Origins & 5 Keys / 習俗源起與五大特色

Origins / 起源

To stop a devastating plague, residents set up altars to pray to Pak Tai to end disasters.

為遏止疫症蔓延，島民設立祭壇向北帝祈求消災解難。

1. Decoration of venue / 會場佈置

Setting up grand altars (for deities to overlook), erecting three giant bamboo bun towers covered with thousands of lucky "Ping On" buns, and displaying large colorful paper deity statues at the ritual venue.

在打醮會場，主辦當局設立了多個大型神棚（供奉神祇），並設立三座掛滿了幾千個「平安包」的巨大包山。此外，會場內亦會展示巨大的彩色紙紮神像。

2. worship activities / 祭祀活動

Conducting elaborate Taoist rituals, presenting animal and fruit food offerings to Pak Tai, and strictly observing a 3-day vegetarian fast (vegetarian eating) island-wide to purify the community.

大會會舉行道教祭祀儀式、向北帝獻祭（如原隻豬和羊等祭品）。島上所有居民必須一連三天吃素，這是齋戒期，全島店舖亦不會提供肉食。

3. carnival parade / 飄色會景巡遊

The vibrant Piu Sik (Floating Colors) Parade. Young children dress up as popular mythical deities or modern political and social figures, standing "suspended" high up in the air on hidden metal frames carried through the streets.

極具特色且熱鬧非凡的飄色巡遊。年幼的小朋友會裝扮成傳統神話的神仙、或現代社會的時事焦點人物，站在一根幼鐵梗上、離地半空「懸空」在街上巡遊。

4. spirit assuaging ceremony / 祭幽儀式

Performing traditional rituals to pacify and feed wandering ghosts, culminating in the burning of a massive paper-crafted statue of the Ghost King (Da Shi Wang) to safely guide spirits back to the spiritual realm.

舉行盛大儀式來超渡、安撫與拜祭四方的孤魂野鬼。儀式的最高潮是焚燒巨大的紙紮大士王（鬼王），象徵將所有鬼神送離長洲、帶走疾病與噩運。

5. bun scrambling / 搶包山

The signature grand finale event of climbing high bamboo bun towers at midnight to gather lucky Ping On buns.

在打醮的最後一晚午夜十二點，大批參賽者會攀上高聳的包山，爭相奪取平安包，搶得越多代表越幸運。

🕌 Topic 3: Rise of Islamic Civilisation / 伊斯蘭文明的興起與中古歐亞交流

Part 1: Three Major Religions / 三大主要宗教

Nowadays, there are three most influential religions, including Buddhism, Christianity and Islam. Islam began to spread in early AD 7th century in the Arabian Peninsula in West Asia. It has developed into the second greatest religion in the world, next only to Christianity. There are almost two billion followers of Islam, making up about one fifth of the world's total population.

現今世界影響最大的宗教有三個，包括佛教、基督宗教和伊斯蘭教。在上述三大宗教當中，伊斯蘭教在公元7世紀前期於西亞的阿拉伯半島開始傳播，發展至今已成為僅次於基督宗教的世界第二大宗教，信徒數目接近二十億，佔世界總人口超過五分之一。

Part 2: Life of Prophet Muhammad / 穆罕默德的傳教生平

In AD 630, Muhammad led his army from Medina to retake Mecca, wiped out the idols in the Kaaba, and built a mosque around the Kaaba, called the Forbidden Mosque. Since then, Mecca has become the most important holy city of Islam.

After Muhammad retook Mecca, most Arabian tribes converted to Islam, and the Arabian Peninsula was unified. In AD 632, Muhammad died in Medina.

公元630年，穆罕默德率軍由麥地那重奪麥加，掃除天房內的偶像，並圍繞天房建立清真寺，稱為「禁寺」。從此，麥加成為伊斯蘭教最重要的聖城。

穆罕默德重奪麥加後，阿拉伯各部落大多皈依伊斯蘭教，整個阿拉伯半島大體上歸於統一。在公元632年，穆罕默德在麥地那逝世。

  • Under the efforts of Islamic rulers, the Koran was later complied. It is one of the important religious texts of Islam. The Arabic word koran means 'recite'.在伊斯蘭統治者的努力下，《古蘭經》編纂成冊，作為伊斯蘭教重要的經典。「古蘭」在阿拉伯文中， 意為「誦讀」。
  • The Hadith, which recorded Muhammad's sayings and teachings, served as the basis of behaviour among Muslims.另外，「聖訓」記載了穆罕默德的言行，是穆斯林言行舉止的依據。
Part 3: Basic Islamic Teachings / 伊斯蘭教基本教義詳解

📜 The "Six Articles of Faith" / 伊斯蘭教的「六信」：

  • Believe in Allah: Allah is the only highest creator. He knows about everything. There is no other god but Allah.信真主：阿拉是至高無上、獨一無二、全知全能的造物主。阿拉之外沒有別的神。
  • Believe in angels: Allah created the angels to give people messages and teachings from Allah.信天使：天使由阿拉創造，負責向人類傳達阿拉的啟示。
  • Believe in religious texts: Muslims must follow the teachings from the Koran and other religious texts.信經典：穆斯林必須遵從《古蘭經》及其他經典的教誨。
  • Believe in prophets: Allah sent Muhammad to this world. Muhammad is the greatest and the last prophet.信先知：穆罕默德是阿拉所派遣最偉大和最後一位先知。
  • Believe in the Judgment Day: When the end of the world comes, Allah will revive all the dead and judge them. Good people will go to heaven, and bad people will go to hell.信末日：末日來臨時，阿拉會復活所有死者並對他們進行審判。善人會進入天堂，惡人會被打入地獄。
  • Believe in predestination: All deeds of human beings are predestined by the will of Allah.信前定：人類所有的行為，都是根據阿拉旨意早已預定。

🖐️ The "Five Pillars of Islam" / 伊斯蘭教的「五功」：

  • Shahada: Declare faith towards the truth that Allah is the real god and Muhammad is the messenger of Allah.唸功：宣示自己堅信阿拉是真神，穆罕默德則是阿拉的信使。
  • Salah: Five daily prayers, kneeling and worshipping towards the direction of Mecca.拜功：每天祈禱五次，而且須向着麥加的方向跪拜。
  • Sawm: Fasting for a month every year (in the ninth month of Islamic calendar). Muslims should not eat or drink from dawn to sunset.齋功：每年的齋戒月 (伊斯蘭曆的9月)，不能在破曉至日落飲食。
  • Zakat: Muslims with excess wealth, clothes and food should help the needy and the poor.課功：凡有盈餘財富、衣服、食物等，必須贈予有需要的人或貧民。
  • Hajj: Pilgrimage to Mecca at least once in life time when personal finance and health permit.朝功：在經濟及健康許可下，一生至少往麥加朝聖一次。

⚠️ Prohibitions & Modesty Codes / 日常生活與服飾禁忌：

Besides the 'Six Articles of Faith and Five Pillars of Islam', Muslims must not eat pork and all unclean food, drink wine, gamble and practise usury. Adult Muslim women should dress modestly in the presence of unrelated men, wearing a headscarf and a robe to cover their hair, neck, and most parts of their body.

穆斯林不能吃豬肉和一切不潔淨的食物，禁止飲酒、賭博、放高利貸等。成年穆斯林女性在非親屬男性面前應穿著得體，須穿戴頭巾、罩袍，遮蔽頭髮、頸部與身體大部分部位。
